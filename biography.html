EARLY LIFE

Where It All Started

Lionel Andrés Messi was born on June 24, 1987, in Rosario, Argentina. From a young age, he showed an incredible passion and natural ability for football.

He grew up playing football with his family and joined the youth team of Newell's Old Boys. Even as a child, Messi stood out because of his speed, control, and ability with the ball.

A BIG OPPORTUNITY

A New Beginning in Barcelona

As a child, Messi was diagnosed with a growth hormone deficiency. His family faced the challenge of paying for his treatment.

At just 13 years old, he moved to Spain after FC Barcelona offered to support his development and treatment. This opportunity changed the direction of his life.

THE RISE OF A LEGEND

From Young Talent to Superstar

Messi developed through Barcelona's famous La Masia academy. He made his first-team debut in 2004 and quickly became one of the most exciting young players in football.

His incredible dribbling, creativity, and goal-scoring ability helped him become one of the most recognized athletes in the world.
