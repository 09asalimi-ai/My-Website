A WINNING CAREER

Built on Greatness

Throughout his career, Lionel Messi has collected some of football's most important individual awards and team trophies. His success reflects years of dedication, hard work, and extraordinary talent.

8 Ballon d'Or Awards

Messi has won the Ballon d'Or eight times, recognizing him as the world's outstanding footballer on eight occasions.

World Cup Champion

In 2022, Messi captained Argentina to victory at the FIFA World Cup in Qatar, achieving one of his biggest career dreams.

Copa América Titles

Messi helped Argentina win the Copa América in 2021 and again in 2024.

Champions League Titles

During his time with Barcelona, Messi won the UEFA Champions League four times.

Barcelona's Top Scorer

Messi became FC Barcelona's all-time leading scorer, setting a remarkable standard for future players.

Olympic Gold Medal

Messi won an Olympic gold medal with Argentina at the 2008 Beijing Olympic Games.
