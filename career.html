THE JOURNEY

Four Chapters of Greatness

Lionel Messi's career has taken him across different leagues and countries. From becoming a superstar at Barcelona to representing Argentina on the biggest international stage, every chapter has added to his story.

2004 – 2021

FC Barcelona

Messi made his first-team debut for Barcelona in 2004. He became the club's leading star, winning numerous trophies and building an unforgettable partnership with teammates. He became Barcelona's all-time leading scorer before leaving in 2021.

2021 – 2023

Paris Saint-Germain

In 2021, Messi joined Paris Saint-Germain in France. He played alongside world-class teammates and helped the club win two French league titles during his time in Paris.

2023 – PRESENT

Inter Miami

Messi joined Inter Miami in 2023, bringing worldwide attention to the club and Major League Soccer. He helped the team win the Leagues Cup in his first season.

ARGENTINA

International Career

Messi represented Argentina throughout his international career. His achievements include winning the 2021 Copa América, the 2022 FIFA World Cup, and the 2024 Copa América.
